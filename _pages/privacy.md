This is a personal academic website. It is built to collect as little as possible about
its visitors.

## What this site does not do

- **No cookies** and no other browser storage (local storage, session storage, IndexedDB).
- **No analytics, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or advertising.**
- **No JavaScript** at all.
- **No third-party resources.** Fonts, icons, styles and images are all served from this
  domain. Nothing is loaded from CDNs, Google Fonts, social networks or any other external
  service, so visiting a page does not contact anyone except the server hosting this site.
- **No embeds, forms or comment systems.**

These guarantees are enforced technically: every page ships with a Content Security Policy
that forbids the browser from running scripts or loading anything from other domains, and a
referrer policy that prevents your browser from telling other sites that you came from here.

## Hosting

This site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/), a service of GitHub, Inc.
According to [GitHub's documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection),
when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored by GitHub
for security purposes. I do not have access to these logs and do not use them. GitHub acts
under its own [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## External links

Links to other websites (for example Google Scholar, ORCID, LinkedIn or publishers) are plain
links. Nothing from those sites is loaded until you click one, after which their own privacy
policies apply.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR (and the Swiss FADP) you have the right to access, rectify and erase personal data, to restrict or
object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since this
site does not itself collect personal data, requests about server logs should be addressed to
GitHub. For anything else, feel free to contact me through the links on the
[home page]({{ '/' | relative_url }}).
