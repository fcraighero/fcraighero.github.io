Our paper [*Interpretable inflammation landscape of circulating immune cells*](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-04126-3)
is now published in *Nature Medicine*.

We built a single-cell atlas of more than 6.5 million peripheral blood mononuclear cells from
1,047 patients across 19 diseases, spanning infections, immune-mediated inflammatory diseases
and cancer. From it, we learned a model of inflammation in circulating immune cells and laid the
groundwork for classifying patients with unsupervised and explainable machine learning.

The atlas is shared as a resource for the community.
Read the paper: [doi:10.1038/s41591-025-04126-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-025-04126-3).
