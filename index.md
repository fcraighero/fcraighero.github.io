I am a scientist at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/), where I work at the intersection of
machine learning and biology with the [Signal Processing Laboratory 2 (LTS2)](https://lts2.epfl.ch/)
and the [Naef Lab](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/naef-lab/).
My research focuses on deep learning models for molecular and cellular data, from predicting
ribosome dynamics along mRNA sequences to building and querying single-cell atlases of
inflammation, and on making these models more reliable and interpretable.

Before joining EPFL, I obtained my PhD in Computer Science at the
[University of Milano-Bicocca](https://www.unimib.it/), with a thesis on anomaly detection
in deep learning, covering the interpretability of activation patterns, adversarial example
detection and out-of-distribution detection. During my PhD, I spent six months as a visiting
researcher at IBM Research Zurich.
